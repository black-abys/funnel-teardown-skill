# Funnel Teardown Skill

An AI agent skill (`SKILL.md`) for studying competitor sales funnels **ethically** and building your **own** version: a quiz landing page, Instagram DM keyword scripts, follow-up messages and a lead-magnet outline.

**Created by Katlego Mokwena** (Creative Director & Brand Strategist, [CortexLab](https://cortexlab.co.za)).
Inspired by a reel from [@angus.sewell](https://www.instagram.com/angus.sewell/) about reverse-engineering coaching funnels.

## What it does
1. Learns your brand from your site
2. Researches 4–6 competitors from **public sources only**
3. Tears down their hook, offer, lead magnet, qualification, follow-up and sales handoff
4. Builds an original, on-brand funnel for you
5. Hands everything back for your approval. Nothing is published or sent without your OK.

## Principles
- Public information only. No logins and no fake form submissions.
- No invented testimonials or stats. Marked placeholders are used instead.
- Adapt patterns. Never copy designs or copy word for word.
- The user approves before anything is published or sent.

## Install
Copy `SKILL.md` into a folder in your agent's skills directory, e.g.
- Claude: `~/.claude/skills/funnel-teardown/SKILL.md`
- Cursor / other agents: your project's skills folder (e.g. `.cursor/skills/funnel-teardown/SKILL.md`)

Then ask: *"Tear down funnels in my niche and build me a quiz funnel."*
